# Privacy Policy for MyWinamp

**Last updated:** November 10, 2026

MyWinamp is an open-source retro media player desktop application.

## Data Collection and Usage
- **Personal Data:** MyWinamp does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information to external servers.
- **YouTube API & OAuth:** MyWinamp connects directly from your local computer to Google and YouTube Music APIs using OAuth 2.0 to access your playlists and playback audio. Authentication tokens are stored locally on your machine and are never shared with any third party.
- **Third-Party Services:** Audio and video streaming data is retrieved directly from YouTube / Google servers in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, you may open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/aisoldati/MyWinamp).
